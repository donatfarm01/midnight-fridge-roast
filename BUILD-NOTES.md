Build notes for the Midnight Fridge Roast entry.

The bounty asks for one line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open
after midnight, judged pairwise by a deadpan comedy judge. The judge weighs
being on topic first, then a real joke with a surprising turn, then
tightness, then making the midnight refusal itself the target, then sounding
like something a person would actually say out loud at the kitchen door.

The cliche radar ruled out the lane most entries will take: flat complaints
about being hungry, the fridge having a bedtime, the fridge judging the
owner, and the nightclub bouncer analogy. Those lines grumble about smart
homes instead of roasting the petty rule.

Three contrasting concepts were scored on hook, originality, sponsor fit and
clarity. The first concept framed the fridge as a clerk citing policy. The
second flipped the protection around: the fridge locks the food away because
the leftovers are safer with it than with the owner. The third spoke in the
fridge's own voice, as an apology note. The winning concept was the legal
gag: the fridge took out a restraining order against the owner's hunger.
It scored highest because the restraining order is a surprising turn, it
makes the refusal itself the joke, it is a single tight line that reads
cleanly aloud, and it is unmistakably about a smart fridge refusing to open
after midnight.

The entry program is a small Python script that validates the line against
the gate, one to two hundred characters, single line, not only white space,
and writes it to the output entry file. The repository carries the three
live make.fun badge lines with the slug-prefixed rank and reward image
addresses, an MIT license file carrying the SPDX identifier line, and the
owner file holding the primary wallet.
